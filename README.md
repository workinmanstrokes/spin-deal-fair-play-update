## Math transparency

The disclosed prize table lives in `src/slot.js` (`PRIZE_TABLE`). The simulation harness in `src/simulate.js` runs at least 10,000 logged slot spins plus blackjack/roulette sessions and compares actual RTP and hit frequency against the prize table. Results are published on the in-app Math Transparency screen and in `docs/sim-results.md` (generated by `npm run sim`).

Target (open question — confirm before retuning): RTP and hit frequency ranges are not stated in the prize table; the retune aims to remove the "fun at first, then you can't win" pattern, i.e. a steady hit frequency with no long dry spells baked into the weights.
